# HR-ONB-001: Pre-Joining Document Submission Policy

**Policy Code:** HR-ONB-001  
**Version:** 1.2  
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01  
**Target Audience:** EMPLOYEE, HR_ADMIN, HR_REVIEWER  
**Scope:** All Departments, All Locations  

---

## 1. Purpose and Overview
This policy outlines the mandatory document submission requirements for all incoming employees across all global and regional delivery centers. Adherence to these guidelines ensures regulatory compliance, statutory identity validation, and seamless employee profile creation.

## 2. Mandatory Pre-Joining Documents
All new joiners must submit digital copies of the following documents no later than 5 business days prior to their official joining date.

### 2.1 Government Identity Verification
Every employee must provide one primary government-issued photo identity proof:
- **India:** Permanent Account Number (PAN) Card AND Aadhaar Card.
- **US:** Social Security Card or Passport.
- **Other Geographies:** Valid National ID Card or International Passport.
*Condition:* The document must not be expired on or before the joining date. Name and Date of Birth on the identity document must match the offer letter and HR profile.

### 2.2 Proof of Address
Employees must submit one document verifying their permanent and current residence:
- Utility bill (electricity, water, piped gas) issued within the last 3 months.
- Valid Passport or Voter ID.
- Bank statement with residential address issued within the last 60 days.

### 2.3 Relieving Letter and Experience Documentation
For experienced hires (Grade L3 and above):
- Relieving letter and formal service certificate from the most recent previous employer.
- Last 3 months' salary slips or compensation statements.
*Fresh graduates (Grade L1/L2) are exempt from this requirement.*

## 3. Submission Guidelines & Formatting
- **Accepted File Types:** PDF, JPEG, PNG. Word documents and ZIP archives are strictly prohibited.
- **File Size Limitation:** Individual file size must not exceed 10 MB.
- **Legibility Standards:** Scanned copies must be high-resolution (minimum 300 DPI) with all four corners visible. Blurry, cropped, glare-obscured, or password-protected files will be rejected.

## 4. Document Verification & Rejection Handling
- All submitted documents undergo two-stage automated and human reviewer verification within 48 hours of submission.
- When a document fails validation (e.g. expired document, name mismatch, illegible text), the status is updated to **Correction Requested**.
- The employee receives an immediate notification stating the exact reason for correction and a direct link to re-upload.
- Documents marked **Accepted** are locked and cannot be modified without HR Admin intervention.
