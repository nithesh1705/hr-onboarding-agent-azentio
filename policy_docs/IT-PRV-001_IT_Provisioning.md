# IT-PRV-001: IT Asset Provisioning and System Access Policy

**Policy Code:** IT-PRV-001  
**Version:** 1.4  
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01  
**Target Audience:** IT_ADMIN, HR_ADMIN, EMPLOYEE  
**Scope:** Engineering, Product, Sales, Design, Corporate  

---

## 1. Laptop and Hardware Standards
Equipment allocation depends on department and seniority level:
- **Engineering & Product:** Apple MacBook Pro 16" (M3 Pro, 32GB RAM, 512GB SSD) or Dell XPS 15 (i9, 32GB RAM, 1TB SSD).
- **Sales, Marketing, HR, Finance:** Apple MacBook Air 13" (M3, 16GB RAM, 512GB SSD) or Lenovo ThinkPad T14.
- **Delivery Timeline:** Laptops are dispatched 4 business days before joining date for remote joiners, or placed at the local office IT Helpdesk for in-person joiners.

## 2. IT Account Creation & Prerequisites
- **HR Record Precondition:** The IT department will NOT create an corporate Google Workspace account (@company.com) until the HR Admin formally confirms employee profile creation in the Core HR database.
- **Single Sign-On (SSO):** Credentials are sent to the employee's personal email 24 hours prior to Day One, with one-time activation password valid for 48 hours.

## 3. Internal IT Fulfillment Workflows [INTERNAL - IT_ADMIN / HR_ADMIN ONLY]
- IT Admin SLA for hardware imaging: 48 hours from HR Record creation trigger.
- Asset tagging requirement: Every laptop must be registered in the IT Asset Management CMDB with barcode, serial number, and assigned MAC address before release.
- Access bundle matrix: Developers receive automatic group assignment to `eng-github-standard`, `eng-slack-channels`, and `aws-dev-sandbox` only after SEC-POL-001 acknowledgement is logged.
