# HR-BGV-001: Background Verification (BGV) Policy and Protocols

**Policy Code:** HR-BGV-001  
**Version:** 2.0  
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01  
**Target Audience:** EMPLOYEE, HR_ADMIN, HR_REVIEWER, COMPLIANCE  
**Scope:** All Departments, All Locations  

---

## 1. Background Check Mandate
All offers of employment are contingent upon successful completion of background checks conducted by our designated global verification partner.

## 2. Components of the Check
- **Identity & Criminal Record Check:** Multi-jurisdiction criminal record history and identity validation.
- **Employment History:** Verification of past 5 years of employment, including designations, tenure dates, and reason for separation.
- **Address & Reference Verification:** In-person or digital geographic address check and 2 professional supervisory references.

## 3. Dependency on IT and Production Access
- Background verification initiation occurs immediately upon submission of required identity proofs.
- An interim clear background check status (**BGV-GREEN** or **BGV-AMBER-EXCEPTION**) is a hard prerequisite before IT can activate production repository credentials or administrative network access.
- Any major discrepancy (**BGV-RED**) escalates directly to the Head of HR Compliance for formal adjudication.
