# HR-ONB-002: Academic Qualifications and Education Verification Policy

**Policy Code:** HR-ONB-002  
**Version:** 1.1  
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01  
**Target Audience:** EMPLOYEE, HR_ADMIN, HR_REVIEWER  
**Scope:** Engineering, Product, Analytics, Finance, Operations  

---

## 1. Objective
To establish guidelines for validating educational credentials and professional certifications required for role eligibility.

## 2. Minimum Qualification Requirements
- **Engineering & Product (Grades L2-L6):** Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Electronics, or equivalent engineering discipline.
- **Analytics & Data Science:** Degree in Mathematics, Statistics, Economics, Computer Science, or related quantitative field.
- **Finance & Compliance:** Degree in Commerce, Accounting, or certified CA / CPA / CFA credentials.

## 3. Required Documentation
- Consolidated or semester-wise mark sheets for all completed degree programs.
- Final Degree Certificate or Official Provisional Passing Certificate from an accredited institution.
- In cases of ongoing higher education, a bonafide letter from the university controller of examinations stating expected completion.

## 4. Discrepancy Protocol
Any falsification, unauthorized modification, or unaccredited institution degree will result in immediate disqualification and revocation of the employment offer.
