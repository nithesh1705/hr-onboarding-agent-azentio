# HR-DAY1-001: First-Day Office Arrival and Orientation Guide

**Policy Code:** HR-DAY1-001  
**Version:** 1.3  
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01  
**Target Audience:** EMPLOYEE, HR_ADMIN, FACILITIES  
**Scope:** Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi, Remote

## 1. General Day One Instructions
All new joiners are expected to report at 09:30 AM local time. Dress code is smart business casual. Please carry original physical copies of submitted government ID documents and degree certificates for one-time visual verification.

## 2. Location-Specific Arrival Details

### 2.1 Bengaluru Campus
- **Address:** Tower B, 5th Floor, Global Technology Park, Bellandur Outer Ring Road, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560103.
- **Reporting Contact:** Reception Desk - Gate 2 (Contact: Anand Kumar, Facilities Lead).
- **Arrival Procedure:** Collect temporary visitor badge at Ground Floor Security by presenting your Offer Letter digital PDF. Report to 5th Floor Cafeteria for the 10:00 AM Welcome Breakfast.
- **IT Handover:** IT Helpdesk Bay 3 at 11:30 AM for laptop handover and password setup.

### 2.2 Mumbai Center
- **Address:** Floor 12, One World Center, Tower 2, Lower Parel (West), Mumbai, Maharashtra 400013.
- **Reporting Contact:** Front Office Desk (Contact: Priya Deshmukh, Office Admin).
- **Arrival Procedure:** Visitor pass issued at the West Wing Turnstile. Joining batch meets in the Sapphire Boardroom at 09:45 AM.
- **IT Handover:** Desk delivery by IT Operations team during the afternoon session.

### 2.3 Delhi NCR Office
- **Address:** 8th Floor, DLF Cyber City, Building 10, DLF Phase 2, Gurugram, Haryana 122002.
- **Reporting Contact:** Concierge Desk (Contact: Vikram Malhotra, Facilities Executive).
- **Arrival Procedure:** Security verification at Tower Reception using Government Photo ID. Orientation begins at 10:00 AM in the North Hub training room.

## 3. First-Day Orientation Schedule
- 09:30 AM - 10:00 AM: Reception, badging, and welcome kit collection
- 10:00 AM - 11:30 AM: Executive Welcome & Company Culture orientation
- 11:30 AM - 01:00 PM: IT Laptop setup, SSO activation, and workstation allocation
- 01:00 PM - 02:00 PM: Welcome lunch with hiring manager and buddy
- 02:00 PM - 04:00 PM: HR benefits enrollment, medical insurance briefing, and policy Q&A
- 04:00 PM - 05:00 PM: Departmental team introduction
